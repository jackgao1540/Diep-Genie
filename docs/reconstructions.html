One clock, three views. Playback loops at 10 sampled frames per second. Use ← / → to step and Space to play while the viewer is focused. Center ×2 changes the crop, not the model’s output.

Reconstruction, not prediction. The tokenizer sees each displayed input frame and compresses/rebuilds it. This is not a generated future, a playable world, or a test of learned movement, aiming or firing.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Follow the small things.

Compare the barrel outline and direction, the player’s color, projectile births and food shapes. Average image error can improve while these details disappear.

HOW TO READ THE EXAMPLES

Public clips are a small, already-published selection with source credits. Training examples and held-out validation examples are labeled separately. The full fixed validation evaluation is available through a local comparison file, without publishing its footage.
