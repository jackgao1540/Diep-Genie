WHAT YOU’RE LOOKING AT

Real footage.
Traceable decisions.

All clips here are recorded gameplay, not model predictions. The full dataset remains local; this site shares a compact, read-only selection.

01

Retain the useful footage

Screen for visible players, clean crops, continuity and ordinary shooting classes. Preserve source timestamps and hashes.

02

Inspect what gets lost

Compare source crops with 128px frames. Small projectiles, tank details and shape edges are important fidelity checks.

03

Learn from the exclusions

Frozen images need holds. Low-motion screening can also reject active gameplay; the recovered examples show why a threshold is not a final verdict.

NEXT CHAPTER

From clean footage to a tested model.

The numerical training fix is verified, and local reconstruction evaluation is complete. Average image error improves, but player and barrel retention still falls short. The next work is preserving those details before training and evaluating independent movement, aiming and firing. No playable model exists yet.
